Lessons recorded along the way

  1. Never crown on stand-dummy numbers; rank by chain strength at the DEPLOY rung (ms_g6_sp1: dummy record, zero shines vs a human — overfit to the frozen dummy).
  2. Never deploy at an untrained delay-id (id4 untrained → bare d4 collapses chaining; override to 3).
  3. The eval opponent is part of the input distribution — never compare across dummies; YS claims need n>=8 run-level buckets.
  4. Chain skill lives on transient peaks; final loss anti-correlates with behavior → gate-sweep every epoch; run-to-run variance dominates knobs (replicates first).
  5. Delay-0 LABEL LEAK (GOTCHA #113): Slippi records the input on the frame it produced; delay-0 training learns to continue states, never initiate. Labels are now causal by construction.
  6. Drill delayed labels were the recorded future (policy's own broken trajectory), not the expert's — every DAgger round since g19 trained against conflicts; fixed by phase-indexed label_ahead, off-loop labels DROP (hold loses).
  7. Exposure bias / absorbing states (crouch absorber): one frame of slip lands in unseen states; coverage (synth, DAgger from own rollouts) is the lever, not features.
  8. Mode-seeking decodes die live (argmax buttons, mode-of-N ×2, critic argmax selector) — L9, do not retest; sample at T=1.0.
  9. Mix damage is invisible to teacher-forced metrics; val and the recovery tail are decoupled — gate epochs on closed-loop rows, not val.
  10. Labels off the policy manifold teach the teacher's habits (random-program oracle failed fingerprint); policy-guided search + control arm + fingerprint gate.
  11. Every retrain carries ALL validated mixes; preserve absolute shares (dilution regressed convC3); declare a new round for a new budget.
  12. Count a weighting arm's dose before launching (edge-window arm lifted 9 presses).
  13. Port/opponent corpus bugs (#107, E1): 43% of fox_gen_v1 demos were the opponent; always --select-character-port with opponent remap.
  14. Frozen-ditto PPO win rate saturates; an opponent pool and a human are the honest tests; 100% = exploit shape.
  15. Harness rung law: suite --response-delay = id+2; one knob --reaction-delay k everywhere; run a known-pass control before reading a new harness.
  16. Pre-register predictions; sync pools cannot measure offense (async is the offense rung); no mix during any live beam.
