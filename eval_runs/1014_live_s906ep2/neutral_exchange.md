# Neutral-exchange outcomes (C1)

Sets: expert: 400 files · bot: 30 files. Exchange = 30 f of mutual
neutral, then the first hit within 900 f. Subject = port 1 (expert: port 1).
"whiff punish" = the loser had an attack state active within 20 f of being hit.
Expert sets are Fox dittos between two strong players, so the expert subject's win rate
is ~50% by construction — the bot's number is read against that.
