## THE CLASSIFIER LESSON (Bradley's review, 08-10)

The percent<20 SD heuristic (GameEvents + analyze_behavior) is wrong
both ways: spikes/edgeguards kill at low percent (false SD), dash-offs
happen at high percent (false KO). The harvester therefore uses a
TRAJECTORY classifier: death anchor = grounded->airborne transition
off a toward-edge DASHING/RUNNING frame at the teeter, followed by a
stock loss with NO hitstun in between (untouched fall — excludes
edgeguards at any percent). Backport to GameEvents still owed (see
tasks). Naming rule from the same review: the term "gimp" is banned
(slur); "edgeguard" names the SITUATION of contesting a recovery,
never a death kind. Death kinds stay two-way :sd/:ko (trajectory).
Byproduct doc: docs/planning/SITUATION_LABELS.md (~50 mechanically
detectable situation labels, expansive by design — Bradley wants a
winnowing pass later).
