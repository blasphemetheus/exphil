## 5. Gotchas / operational law (new this session; older in HANDOFF_2026-08-30b §5 and GOTCHAS.md)

  size, port map, embed layout) states its cache budget and checks
  `df` first; stale cache generations move to /data; a chunk without
  its `.manifest` is garbage.
- **No inline multi-line scripts under `systemd-run`** — the
  systemd/devenv quoting stack ate `${name}` while `$name` survived
  (all Leg S `--out` files landed on one hidden `.md`; recovered from
  logs, L3). Script FILES only. Never edit a `.sh` a running bash is
  executing.
- **Frozen-trunk fits are attribution constraints**: a capability
  living in few parameters (the AR tf-wire) erodes whenever the big
  parameter mass trains beside it. Measure L_cond
  (`scripts/coincidence_probe.exs`, ~4 min) after ANY joint training
  of an AR head.
- **Nx.Tensor satisfies `is_map/1`** — a bare `is_map` clause swallows
  tensors; tensor clauses first, map clauses guarded `not is_struct`
  (bit both the transplant loader and its test).
- `loop_report` now excludes <150 KB stubs (`--min-bytes`) and prints
  mean~median cells; session-dir means were poisoned by one degenerate
  game (the D2 331-taunts artifact).
- Cross-instrument absolute match rates (interp_passk vs critic ladder)
  are NOT comparable — different match rules/denominators; only
  within-table differences mean anything.
- `--select-character-port` is streaming-pipeline-only (loud error
  otherwise); requires `--train-character`.
