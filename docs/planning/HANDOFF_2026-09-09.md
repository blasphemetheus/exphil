## 2. The finding (read GOTCHAS.md #113 for the measured version)

Slippi records each frame's controller on the frame whose post-update
state it PRODUCED. Training at label delay 0 paired state[t] with
controller[t] — a target already visible in the state. The model learns
to CONTINUE states and never to INITIATE them. That one mechanism
reproduces the entire fox_gen pathology board: 6.6x WAIT dwell, dash-out
1000x under-predicted (0.0002 vs experts 0.13-0.29), WALK_SLOW>STANDING
loops, half-input exits, and the jab chain's leaked A-press. The
multishine line trained at {2,3} = causal, which is why it has
initiative. Every "calibrated" probe verdict before 09-09 read through
the leak because same-frame labels are ~0 for experts and model alike.

v16e (v16a twin + causal labels, 2 epochs, 2,500 files) fixed it at the
source: p(dash-out|WAIT) 0.0002 -> 0.11-0.15, headless idle 0.188 ->
0.02, dwell 34.7 -> 3.2. NOT fixed: the jab chain (model 0.21 vs expert
~1%/frame in the chain window) — that is the action-frame-resolution
lever (§6).

## 3. What changed structurally today (INVARIANTS.md is the ledger)

All 11 items are structural. The two that change how you launch things:

**Item 1 (maximal form, Bradley's call) — LABEL NUMBERING CHANGED.**
`Peppi.to_training_frames` always emits causal pairs (`causal_pairs/1`:
state[t] with raw controller[t+1]; last frame dropped). `--frame-delay`
(streaming/bptt) and `--action-delay` (standard) are REACTION delay on
top; default 0 = causal; the leak is unrepresentable (guard and
`--allow-leaky-labels` deleted). **Old delay d == new d-1**: v16e's
`--frame-delay 1` == today's default; drills' `--action-delay 2` == 1;
`--multi-delay "2,3"` == "1,2". `ExPhil.Data.LabelConvention` owns the
mapping: checkpoints stamp `label_convention: :causal`; unstamped =
legacy. **The live `--frame-delay N` flag is NOT renumbered** (it is
Slippi's setting; every deploy card keeps its numbers): live N plays
reaction N-1, so a causal checkpoint at the default deploys at
`--frame-delay 1`, and the Agent derives its delay-id from the
checkpoint convention (causal: N-1, legacy: N; `--delay-id-override`
still wins). Comparability keys count in reaction terms (legacy delay-1
== causal delay-0). Tests: `label_alignment_test` (`:nif`),
`label_convention_test` (the deploy cards as cases).

**Item 3 (middle road) — 70 of 97 backbones are DATA.** A `build:`
recipe + `output:` rule on the `Config.@backbone_specs` row;
`build_temporal_backbone` keeps 25 bespoke clauses and falls through to
`build_from_spec`. `backbone_spec_build_test` constructs all 70.
Adding a uniform architecture = one row.

**Item 4's consequence you will hit:** Peppi-trained checkpoints since
09-09 have NO projectile block (264 dims, not 296). `with_projectiles`
is "requested AND provided" and Peppi never provides, so **you cannot
`--resume` from any pre-09-09 checkpoint on Peppi data** (v1, v2,
v16a-e). v3 is from scratch by decision anyway.

Other items, one line each: 2 flag table -> parser/defaults/docs
(TRAINING.md reference is GENERATED; `FlagDocs.write!()`), 5 ports only
at the boundary (`GameState.subject/opponent`), 6 one inference per
frame (`:new_frame` driven), 7 `%Decode{}` struct, 8 `LossConfig`, 9
`Interp.Labels` (successor-aligned), 10 cache budget, 11 comparability
key (Registry refuses mixed rankings).
