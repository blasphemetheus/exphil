## How the holdout actually fell (method lessons, do not relearn)

The 22b candidates (0x80444964, 0x80479C58) were red herrings born of
TWO false premises, both exposed by the visual plan in one afternoon:

1. **`Probe.navigate!` never picked fox.** Its default `until` is CSS
   ARRIVAL; the "pre-picked" state everyone diffed against was really
   coin-in-hand hovering. Magnified screenshot of the hand sprite
   (fist holding coin vs open hand + placed coin) was the tell.
2. **Probe `tap!` presses land nondeterministically against a
   free-running windowed dolphin.** Step-counted holds can complete in
   sub-frame WALL time (polling steps burn buffered events); sticks
   work (closed-loop over hundreds of steps), buttons silently whiff —

   (press → sleep 250ms → release). Pinned in docs/memory-watch.md.
