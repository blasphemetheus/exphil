## THE FOUR THREADS, AND WHERE EACH STANDS

### 1. Delay / task #20 — the live bottleneck

The 22→4 delta decomposes into a **per-link Bernoulli at the
aerial-shine decision**: air-stretch histograms are bimodal (links are
tight ≤5f or balloon >12f, nothing between), balloon probability ~6x
higher under netplay, and chains die by air-overrun (`:empty_hop`
nearly vanishes). Declared delay-id sets that decision's margin
(p10 −0.52 / +0.09 / +0.25 at ids 2/3/4) — which is why d4 out-chains d3
against a human.

**Lever built and validated offline:** `--margin-weight W` in
`dagger_drill.exs` (`ExPhil.Training.MarginSampling` — upweights the one
aerial-B press-edge frame per cycle). `ms_g12_margin` (weight 3): margins
**−0.23 / +0.28 / +0.33**, id3 3x wider than g10b's, stand held at 423
c423. YS unchanged (3-of-3 collapsed-tier — that axis is seed-unstable
across the whole recipe, see below).

**Owed:** a g12-vs-g10b human netplay A/B (same session, both at d4).
The prediction: g12's wider id3/id4 margins should out-chain g10b.
That single session decides whether `--margin-weight` folds into the
standard recipe.

### 3. Curation / training recipe

Cycle 3b (aligned human snippets) validated the loop and produced g10b.
Cycle 4a/4a2 tried **y-augmentation** (synthetic grounded-at-height
frames): the overdose destroyed the core skill; the safe 12k-frame dose
produced `ms_g11b_yaug` with the **all-time stand record 428.4 c427** —
but n=10 YS replication showed ~80% collapse. Parked. Its one real claim
is the stand record.

**Seed instability is the standing caveat:** a fresh same-recipe GRU
retrain (`bakeoff_gru`) collapsed YS 3-of-3 where g10b went 0-of-11.
The YS axis is not seed-guaranteed by the recipe — g10b itself is solid,
the recipe isn't. Any YS claim needs n≥8 (see EVAL_PROTOCOL).

### 4. Architecture — CLOSED

Bake-off #27 ran GRU vs mamba_2 on the champion recipe: mamba_2 fit the
data identically (loss 0.0016 vs 0.0013) and **failed closed-loop**
(stand 121.8 c1, rung-0 2.91). GRU stays. Architecture is confirmed not
the bottleneck; stop spending here.

## RULES ADOPTED THIS WEEK (all cost real GPU-hours to learn)

1. **n≥8 for any Yoshi's Story claim.** Every n≤3 YS comparison made on
   08-04/05 was noise, in both directions. Deterministic FD/BF runs are
   trustworthy at n=1 (they're bit-identical — stop running 3).
   `docs/guides/EVAL_PROTOCOL.md` has the full protocol: CRN pairing,
   sequential stopping, successive halving, calibration ledger.
2. **Contract mismatches ABORT, not warn** (GOTCHA #86). Cycle 3a's
   action-delay mismatch printed a correct warning into a log nobody
   read and burned 100 GPU-minutes on an invalid checkpoint.
3. **NO-MIX covers eval stages, not just training.** A probe run beside
   a live eval SIGBUS'd it (dual EXLA clients).
4. **GOTCHA #1 keeps biting**: any `term_to_binary` of params/dicts must
   deep-transfer every tensor to BinaryBackend first. Bit us twice more
   this week (dictionary save, planted checkpoint).
5. **Attribution sites must be DECISION-defined**, not animation-defined
   — the qtrace lesson, re-learned in interp. Sites picked on animation
   frames gave weak, sign-mixed validation; decision sites gave
   agreement to ~0.01.
